01 Biography

Birth & Death

Born October 29, 1942 in Daytona Beach, Florida

Died July 4, 1995 in Orlando, Florida — age 52

Famous American painter and TV presenter

His paintings were considered masterpieces — a quiet legacy that endures long after his passing.

02 Beginnings

Early Career

Worked as a carpenter alongside his father

Joined the U.S. Air Force at age 18

Discovered painting while stationed in Alaska — its wilderness changed everything

Alaska's vast, cold landscapes gave Ross his lifelong love of mountains, forests and still waters.

03 Craft

Influence & Technique

Inspired by painter Bill Alexander, who taught him the wet-on-wet oil painting technique

Used his calm, soothing voice and positive attitude to teach painting on television

His style — "happy little trees" — made art accessible to everyone

Wet-on-wet: blending colors directly on the canvas without waiting — spontaneous, natural, alive.
